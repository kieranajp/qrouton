**Cite evidence a reader can follow.**

Anchor each material claim to one representative citation. Use a `path:line` for code and configuration. Use a direct link for an external source. Choose the line that shows the behavior best. One citation carries a claim as well as twenty. Leave out inventories of every matching file and call site. Trust each citation for the detail it holds, and write only what a reader cannot see there.

Mark each claim as verified or as inferred, in the sentence that makes it. Where two sources disagree, report both and leave the disagreement visible.

Report the findings, not the search. Leave out the queries you ran, the paths you ruled out and the order of your reading.
