Find representative examples relevant to the bounded question. Prefer maintained production paths over fixtures or dead code. Explain each pattern, where it is used, meaningful variations, and the surrounding convention. Cite every example you show: comparing them is the deliverable, so the count of examples is yours to choose. Return a compact comparison and gaps; do not choose a solution unless explicitly asked.
